A right square pyramid has a surface area of $100+100\sqrt5$. The area of the base of the pyramid is $100$. What is the height of the pyramid, in units of length?
Λύση

Base area $=100 \Rightarrow$ base side $s=10$.

Total surface area $=$ base area $+\,4\cdot(\text{area of one lateral triangle})=100+4\cdot\frac12\cdot s\cdot l=100+2sl$, where $l$ is the slant height.

$100+2(10)l=100+100\sqrt5 \Rightarrow 20l=100\sqrt5 \Rightarrow l=5\sqrt5$.

The slant height relates to the true height $h$ via: $l^2=h^2+\left(\dfrac{s}{2}\right)^2 \Rightarrow (5\sqrt5)^2=h^2+5^2 \Rightarrow 125=h^2+25 \Rightarrow h^2=100 \Rightarrow h=10$.

Απάντηση: h = 10

The floor and wall are perpendicular to each other, so the beam, the floor, and the wall form a right triangle in which angles $r°$ and $s°$ are complementary: $r+s=90$.

  • A) 123
  • B) 246
  • C) 264
  • D) 957
Λύση

Base area $=144 \Rightarrow$ base side $s=12$.

Slant height $l=\sqrt{h^2+(s/2)^2}=\sqrt{h^2+36}$. Total SA $=$ base $+4\cdot\frac12 sl=144+24l$.

Pyramid 1 ($h=11$): $l_1=\sqrt{121+36}=\sqrt{157}\approx12.530$, SA$_1\approx144+24(12.530)\approx444.72$.

Pyramid 2 ($h=22$): $l_2=\sqrt{484+36}=\sqrt{520}\approx22.804$, SA$_2\approx144+24(22.804)\approx691.28$.

Difference $\approx691.28-444.72\approx246.6$, closest to $246$.

Απάντηση: B — ≈ 246
